Our Philosophy

Tennis belongs
in the neighborhood.

We believe the best tennis communities make room for people to start, return, and grow—at their own pace.

OTP Tennis is rooted in Pinole and designed around skill development, positive instruction, and a welcoming atmosphere. Whether your goal is to learn the basics, build confidence, or find more ways to play, the starting point is the same: opportunity.
